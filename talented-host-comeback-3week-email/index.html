How it works

What you need to know about the 3-week challenge.

  1. Your Talented Host application must be approved between Nov 23 and Dec 13, 2026 (UTC+8).
  2. Your first bonus week begins on the Monday your Talented Host salary plan becomes active. Hosts active on Nov 23 can start that day and receive their first eligible reward on Nov 30.
  3. Weekly rewards are sent every Monday to hosts who meet the requirements.
  4. These campaign rewards are extra and do not replace your regular Talented Host salary.
