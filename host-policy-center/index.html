Levels and elimination

Hosts advance automatically according to the number of weeks in the program.

Weekly eliminationIf Lucky Gift does not meet the threshold for the current level, Talented Host status is removed. The salary returns to the basic Live plan and the host cannot apply for the program again.

Application and activation

  • At least 30 minutes of independent live streaming
  • More than 100 diamonds received during the live stream
  • Available program slots

Application videoNo special effects or heavy filters; clear quality; proper camera distance; at least 30 seconds; clearly demonstrates talent.

ActivationAfter approval, the salary plan starts at 00:00 (UTC+8) on the following Monday.

直播与合规要求

  • 至少参加一次 Livehouse 活动，或完成一次 20 分钟个人直播试播/审核
  • 按平台要求参加指定直播活动
  • 使用合格的封面、头像和昵称；形象整洁得体；与摄像头保持适当距离
  • 使用合适的直播支撑设备并保持充足补光；播放合适的背景音乐；主动与房间内用户互动

违规、警告与资格取消

作弊、欺诈或违反平台规则将立即终止固定薪资政策资格，并可能追加处罚。

表现消极的 Star Host 可能收到警告；一个月内累计 3 次警告，平台可取消 Star Host 资格。平台有权根据主播表现及规则遵守情况审核并调整资格。

Live-streaming and compliance

  • Participate in at least one Livehouse event or complete a 20-minute solo live audition.
  • Participate in specified live events as required by the platform.
  • Use a qualified cover, avatar and nickname; maintain a clean, appropriate appearance and proper camera distance.
  • Use suitable live-streaming support equipment and adequate fill lighting; use appropriate background music; actively interact with room users.

Violations, warnings and cancellation

Fraud, cheating or violation of platform rules immediately terminates the fixed-salary policy and may lead to additional penalties.

Star Hosts with negative performance may receive warnings. Three warnings within one month may result in cancellation of Star Host status. The platform may review and adjust eligibility based on performance and compliance.
