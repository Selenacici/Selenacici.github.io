2. Salary and task calculation

  • Hourly tasks are removed after becoming a Star Host.
  • A maximum of 4 live-streaming hours per day count.
  • Time is counted from 00:00 (UTC+8) on the first day of each week; tasks refresh weekly.
  • Actual salary depends on completed tasks and current level.

3. Application and selection

  • Participate in at least one Livehouse event or complete a 20-minute solo live audition.
  • Participate in specified events as required.
  • All Star Hosts must first be Talented Hosts; eligible J-level hosts apply and the platform manually reviews.

4. Live-streaming standards

  • Qualified cover, avatar and nickname.
  • Clean appearance and proper camera distance.
  • Suitable support/equipment and adequate fill lighting.
  • Appropriate background music and active room interaction.

5. Violations and cancellation

Fraud, cheating or rule violations immediately terminate the fixed-salary policy and may lead to penalties. Negative performance may trigger warnings; three warnings within one month may cancel Star Host status. The platform may review and adjust status based on performance and compliance.
