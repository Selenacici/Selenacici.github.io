Essential policy rules

Apply from Talented Host• Regular hosts cannot apply directly.
• Applicant must be Level 3 in Week 3+.
• Latest 2-week average Lucky Gift must be strictly above 200K diamonds.
• Manual review is required; rejected hosts may reapply anytime.
Salary & live quality• Week resets Monday at 00:00 UTC+8.
• Daily counted live time is capped at 4 hours.
• Rewards are cumulative and paid automatically by the system.
• Complete one Livehouse event or a 20-min trial; use a qualified setup and interact actively.
Risk of losing Star Host status• Cheating, fraud or rule violations end fixed-salary eligibility and may bring further penalties.
• Poor performance may receive warnings.
• Three warnings in one month may remove Star Host status.
