Talented Host Policy

Key Updates in This Policy
  1. The Lucky Gift weekly elimination thresholds for the J, K and L levels, and the rewards paid for completing salary tasks, have been adjusted.
  2. New eligibility requirements and a manual review process have been added for Talented Hosts applying to become Star Hosts.

The Talented Host Program gives high-quality hosts more exposure and better earning opportunities. Talented Hosts are divided into J, K and L levels.

1. Benefits

  • Exclusive profile tag
  • Exclusive live-room badge and traffic boost
  • Talented Host task salary
  • Opportunity to apply for Star Host promotion

2. Application and Activation

  • Complete at least 30 minutes of independent live streaming.
  • Receive more than 100 diamonds during live streaming.
  • The program must have available slots.

Application video: no special effects or heavy filters; clear quality; proper camera distance; at least 30 seconds; clearly demonstrates the host’s talent.

Activation After approval, the salary plan starts at 00:00 (UTC+8) on the following Monday.

3. Levels and Weekly Automatic Advancement

A Talented Host’s level advances automatically each week according to the number of weeks the host has participated in the program. No separate level-up application is required:

  1. Week 1: L Talented Host. Every newly accepted Talented Host starts at Level L.
  2. Week 2: K Talented Host. The host automatically advances to Level K in the second week.
  3. Week 3 and onward: J Talented Host. The host automatically advances to Level J in the third week and remains at Level J unless Talented Host status is removed or the host successfully becomes a Star Host.

Each week, the host must meet the Lucky Gift elimination threshold and complete salary tasks for the host’s current level.

Weekly Elimination Rule

The platform checks each host’s Lucky Gift income against the threshold for the host’s current level every week. A host who does not meet the applicable threshold for that week will be eliminated and lose Talented Host status:

  • An L-level host who receives less than 20K Lucky Gift diamonds that week will be eliminated.
  • A K-level host who receives less than 30K Lucky Gift diamonds that week will be eliminated.
  • A J-level host who receives less than 40K Lucky Gift diamonds that week will be eliminated.

After elimination, all Talented Host benefits are revoked and the host’s salary returns to the basic Live salary plan. An eliminated host may not apply to join the Talented Host Program again.

4. Salary Tasks

Actual salary is based on completed tasks. Only up to 3 hours of live streaming per day count toward task progress; any additional live time does not count for that day. Hosts receive the task rewards assigned to their current J, K or L level.

5. Applying to Become a Star Host

The platform does not accept direct Star Host applications from regular hosts. A host must first join the Talented Host Program and meet all of the following requirements before submitting a Star Host promotion application:

  1. The host must currently be a J Talented Host. This means the host has participated in the Talented Host Program for at least three weeks.
  2. The host’s average Lucky Gift income over the most recent two consecutive weeks must be greater than $20. The calculation is: total Lucky Gift income for the most recent two weeks ÷ 2. The result must be strictly greater than $20.
Eligibility Does Not Mean Automatic Promotion

Meeting the requirements only makes the host eligible to submit a Star Host promotion application. The host must apply to the platform and wait for a manual review.

Manual review: the platform evaluates the host’s overall quality, live-streaming performance, activity, content and interaction quality, compliance with platform rules, and other relevant factors. A host becomes a Star Host only after passing the manual review. If the application is not approved, the host remains a Talented Host and continues to participate in the weekly assessment under the current level rules.
