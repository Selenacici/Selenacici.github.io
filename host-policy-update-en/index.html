Level determination, movement & salary

Your first week confirms your starting level. After that, your weekly results decide your next level.

Your first week is the level-determination week

Your initial level is set from your Lucky Gift result in the previous week. From then on, review happens weekly; each review can move you up or down by no more than one level.

Ready to apply for Star Host?

Star Host is not an automatic upgrade.

Who can apply

  • You are currently a Talented Host Level 3.
  • Your average Lucky Gift income across the latest two continuous weeks is above 200K diamonds.
  • You submit an application to the official channel after meeting both conditions.

Manual review required

Meeting the eligibility requirements lets you apply, but does not guarantee promotion. The platform reviews live performance, content quality, activity, audience interaction, and rule compliance.

Need the full rule details? Open the Talented Host or Star Host policy page in the app. This page is a guide to key changes and does not replace the complete policy.
