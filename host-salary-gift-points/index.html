1. How Gift Points work

Only gifts you receive while streaming count toward Gift Points (GP).

100 Regular Gift diamonds = 1 GPExample: 1,000 diamonds = 10 GP
100 Lucky Gift diamonds = 35 GPExample: 1,000 diamonds = 350 GP

Weekly GP = Regular Gift diamonds ÷ 100 + Lucky Gift diamonds ÷ 100 × 35

  • Gift Points start over each week. Fractions of a point keep adding up.
  • Game bets, streaming time, and other platform activities do not earn Gift Points.
  • Gift Points are used for tasks and levels. They cannot be exchanged, withdrawn, or transferred.

3. Star Host

The app shows Star Host levels as A, B, and C. All newly approved Star Hosts begin at C.

Levels and maximum weekly salary

LevelGP range to keep this levelMax. weekly salary
A Star Host140K GP or more$133
B Star Host35K to under 140K GP$97
C Star Host25K to under 35K GP$75

Weekly level changes

  • A: Earn at least 140K GP to stay at A. Below 140K GP, move to B.
  • B: Earn 140K GP to move up to A; 35K to under 140K GP to stay at B; below 35K GP to move to C.
  • C: Earn 35K GP to move up to B; 25K to under 35K GP to stay at C; below 25K GP to move to Talented Host Level 3 (J).

Your weekly result sets your level for the following week. You can move by no more than one Star Host level in a week.

4. Apply for Star Host

Meeting the requirements lets you apply. Promotion requires manual approval.

Who can apply?

  1. You are a Talented Host at Level 3 (J), in your third week or later.
  2. Your average is at least 70K GP across the latest two complete assessment weeks.

What happens next?

Submit your application for review. The platform also checks your stream quality, activity, content, interactions, and rule compliance. If you are not approved, you remain a Talented Host.
